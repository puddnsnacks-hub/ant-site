Dynamic QR codes

Print once. Point anywhere. Whenever.

Manage tags that are free to wander.

QR Manager gives every printed code a short, permanent address. Where that address sends people can change at any time, from any phone, so the sticker, the flyer and the business card never go back to the printer. Even tags long out in the wild can be controlled, so they stay current.

02

What it does

Change the ending.

A printed code is a promise that can keep up with the news. Here is one lost-cat flyer and what it did for everyone who scanned it, without a single reprint.

  1. Day 1

    Every scan reaches the right page.

    Biscuit is missing. Forty flyers go up around Maple Ave and 7th St, and every scan opens his photo and a number to call.

    example.com/biscuit/lost

    Have you seen Biscuit?

    Orange tabby, white chest and paws. Last seen near Maple Ave and 7th St.

    Call or text (555) 248-7631

  2. Day 11

    Good news travels without a reprint.

    Biscuit strolls home like he went out for milk. One change from the couch, and every flyer on every pole opens a thank-you page instead of a plea.

    example.com/biscuit/home

    Biscuit is home.

    Thank you to everyone on Maple and 7th who looked.

  3. Day 60

    Old print bows out politely.

    The flyers fade and the code retires. Anyone who scans a sun-bleached flyer gets a gentle goodbye, never a dead link or somebody else’s page.

03

In the wild

Rae · throws parties with no address · alley wall

Off the map.

The poster says NO ADDRESS, and it means it. All week the code opens a teaser that just says 9 PM. At 9 sharp a scheduled change swaps in the warehouse map. When the room hits capacity I pause it, so latecomers get a polite “taking a break” page. By sunrise it’s retired, and anybody who comes looking later, uniform or not, finds a code that points nowhere.

08

Features

Ready and working.

Everything on this list works today. The queue below is what comes next, in the order it is worth the most.

Codes

  • Permanent short addresses, never reused
  • Repoint, with history and one-click restore
  • Scheduled changes that fire on the minute
  • Pause, retire and archive, with fallback pages
  • Categories, tags, used-on notes, images and custom fields
  • Library search, filters and keyboard shortcuts

Print

  • Ink, background and transparent styles
  • Compact encoding for a smaller pattern
  • A scannability check with a real decode
  • PNG, SVG and vector PDF export
  • Smallest print size, side by side at one scale

Scans

  • Time, city, device, OS, browser and referrer
  • An explorer with charts, a map and tables
  • Date ranges and period comparison
  • A Home dashboard with what needs attention
  • Your own test scans left out of the count

Keys

  • Passkey sign-in on any number of devices
  • Full backup and restore, images included
